RescuGuard AI is trained on the multi-gigabyte US National Traffic Incidents Dataset (7.7M+ records in data/raw) to benchmark multi-class loss convergence, meteorological dependencies, and roadway risk factors. Standardized digital crash telemetries are currently scarce in Ethiopia; consequently, this system provides an empirical machine learning baseline with a direct drop-in schema ready for immediate deployment across Ethiopian corridors (such as the Addis Ababa Ring Road and the Addis–Djibouti Freight Logistics Artery) once local digital logging is established.
